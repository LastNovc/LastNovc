# Christopher / LastNovc

I’m an independent engineer, researcher, and builder.

I’m drawn to problems that resist tidy categories—the kind that require reading widely, building something concrete, and testing whether the original idea survives contact with reality.

My interests move across software systems, privacy and local-first computing, experimental infrastructure, hardware, research tooling, and the practical work of turning uncertain ideas into things that can be examined.

I care about user ownership, understandable systems, and preserving the evidence behind engineering decisions. I use AI as a collaborator and force multiplier, but I remain responsible for the direction, judgment, and results.

I founded [Saor R&D](https://github.com/Saor-rd) as a home for longer-term software engineering and applied research.

Much of my current work remains private while it is being developed and evaluated. Public work and technical notes will appear when they are ready to stand on their own.
